Laser Hair Removal

At RevivEstetik, laser hair removal is performed exclusively by experienced medical professionals using the advanced Candela GentleMax Pro® system, the gold standard in Laser Hair Removal.

This state-of-the-art device combines dual laser technologies to deliver safe, effective, and long-lasting hair reduction across a wide range of skin tones and hair types.

How It Works

Laser energy is selectively absorbed by the pigment (melanin) within the hair follicle. This energy is converted into heat, effectively disabling the follicle and inhibiting future hair growth, without damaging the surrounding skin.

The integrated Dynamic Cooling Device (DCD™) delivers a burst of cooling before each pulse, enhancing comfort while protecting the skin.


Advanced Technology for Superior Results The Candela GentleMax Pro® utilises two clinically proven wavelengths:

  • Alexandrite Laser (755 nm): Ideal for lighter skin tones (Fitzpatrick I-III), highly effective for finer and lighter hair.
  • Nd:Yag Laser (1064 nm): Designed for darker skin tones (Fitzpatrick IV-VI), offering deeper penetration with enhanced safety.

This dual-wavelength capability allows us to personalise treatments with precision, ensuring optimal results for every patient.

Key Benefits

  1. Clinically Proven Hair Reduction: Significant, long-term reduction in hair growth after a course of treatments
  2. Suitable for All Skin Types: Safe and effective across a broad spectrum of skin tones
  3. Precision & Safety: Advanced technology ensures targeted treatment with minimal risk
  4. Enhanced Comfort: Built-in cooling system minimises discomfort
  5. Fast & Efficient: Larger spot sizes allow quicker treatment of both small and large areas
  6. Minimal Downtime: Resume normal activities immediately after treatment

Common treatment areas

Laser hair removal can be performed on almost any area of the body, including:




  • Face: Upper lip, chin, cheeks, sides
  • Neck, including back of neck and hairline
  • Underarms and arms
  • Chest, abdomen and back
  • Bikini and Brazilian area
  • Legs

Procedure Overview

  1. Consultation: A personalised consultation is essential. We assess your skin type, hair characteristics, and medical history to design a tailored treatment plan.
  2. Preparation: The area should be shaved prior to treatment. Waxing or plucking should be avoided to preserve the hair follicle.
  3. Treatment: The laser delivers precise pulses of energy while the cooling system maintains comfort. Most patients describe the sensation as mild and tolerable.
  4. Aftercare: You will be advised to avoid sun exposure and use SPF regularly. Mild redness may occur but typically resolves within hours.

Results and Maintenance

  • Progressive Results: Hair becomes finer, lighter, and less dense with each session
  • Optimal Outcome: Most patients require a series of 6-8 sessions for best results
  • Long-Term Reduction Up to 80-90% hair reduction is commonly achieved
  • Maintenance: Occasional top-up sessions may be needed over time
  • Safety & Side Effects: Laser hair removal is a safe medical procedure when performed by qualified professionals. Temporary side effects may include:
  • Mild redness or swelling
  • Transient sensitivity
  • Rare, temporary pigmentation changes

All treatments at RevivEstetik are carried out with strict medical protocols to ensure the highest standards of safety and care.
