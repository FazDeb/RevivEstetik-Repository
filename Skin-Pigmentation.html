Pigmentation Treatments

Pigmentation treatments are designed to reduce or eliminate areas of hyperpigmentation, including sunspots, melasma, and uneven skin tone. The aim is to restore a brighter, more uniform complexion by targeting excess melanin within the skin.


Pigmentation treatments are designed to reduce or eliminate areas of hyperpigmentation, including sunspots, melasma, and uneven skin tone. The aim is to restore a brighter, more uniform complexion by targeting excess melanin within the skin.

Treatment Conisderations

  • Type of Pigmentation: The success of treatment depends on whether pigmentation is superficial (such as sun damage) or deeper (such as melasma). Certain modalities are more effective for specific conditions.
  • Skin type: Darker skin tones require careful selection of treatments, as some procedures, particularly aggressive lasers or deep peels, may increase the risk of post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation.
  • Patience & Consistency: Pigmentation correction is a gradual process. Noticeable improvement typically requires consistent treatment over several weeks to months.

Treatment Options

Every patients skin is unique. At consultation, we assess your skin type and pigmentation concerns to design a tailored treatment plan that delivers safe, effective, and long-lasting results.

Topical Treatments

Following a detailed consultation, medical-grade topical treatments may be prescribed. These can be used alone or combined with in-clinic procedures to enhance results and maintain long-term skin health.

Chemical Peels

Chemical peels exfoliate the upper layers of the skin, promoting cell turnover and revealing a clearer, more even complexion.

  • Superficial Peels (e.g., Glycolic Acid, Lactic Acid): Ideal for mild pigmentation and dull skin, these peels provide gradual brightening with minimal downtime.
  • Medium Peels (e.g., TCA - Trichloroacetic Acid): Target more pronounced pigmentation and can penetrate deeper into the skin for more noticeable results.
  • Targeted/Combination Peels: Customised blends of acids are often used to address multiple concerns simultaneously, enhancing efficacy while maintaining safety.

Laser Treatments

Laser technologies precisely target melanin within the skin, breaking down pigment and promoting a more even tone.


PicoWay by Candela: Considered the gold-standard treatment for stubborn and deep pigmentation like melasma


Fractional Laser Resurfacing: Stimulates collagen and skin renewal by treating deeper layers, also highly effective for sun spots


Nd:YAG Laser: Effective for deeper pigmentation, including melasma, whilst also targeting the vascular component

MicroNeedling with PRP (Vampire Facial)

Microneedling creates controlled micro-injuries that stimulate collagen production and accelerate skin renewal. When combined with Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP), it enhances healing and can improve pigmentation and overall skin quality.

Sun Protection

Sun protection is essential in both treating and preventing pigmentation.


Broad-Spectrum Sunscreen (SPF 50+): Protects against UVA and UVB damage - we recommend Isdin Active Unify for Melasma


Daily Use is Crucial: Even minimal sun exposure can worsen pigmentation and reverse treatment results
